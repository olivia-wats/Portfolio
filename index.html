My process
01 / DISCOVER

Define the need.

Meet with stakeholders and key users. Clarify the problem, acceptance criteria, and MVP before exploring solutions.

02 / STRUCTURE

Find the shape.

Wireframe the information and interactions, considering accessibility, responsiveness, and future growth.

03 / DESIGN

Make it tangible.

Develop mockups that make ideas concrete and give stakeholders something specific to respond to. Iterate together.

04 / REFINE

Connect the details.

Prototype interactions, review with developers, document the work, and support QA through implementation.
